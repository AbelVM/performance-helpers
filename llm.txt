# performance-helpers

> A dependency-free toolbox of small, fast, Node/browser libraries: a worker pool,
> an LRU/TTL cache, rate limiters, a concurrency gate, zero-copy buffer helpers,
> a versioned message codec, and a tiny logger. No runtime dependencies, ESM +
> CJS bundles, 1.0.3 published with 2.0 unreleased.

An index, not a tutorial.

**It deliberately does not categorise the guides by subject.** `metaGuide.md`
already does that, by the problem you are trying to solve, and a second taxonomy
here would be a third one to keep in step with the first — which is how
`guides/metaGuide.md` came to carry two "Realtime: framing" sections before they
were merged.

**It does not duplicate their content either.** Every guide line below is a title
and a one-line summary; most open with that guide's own first sentence, truncated
with `…` where it runs long, and a handful are paraphrased where the guide's own
opening was the weaker line. Nothing here is generated, so `npm run docs:claims`
checks the things that can actually break — that each link resolves, and that no
summary has been truncated mid-sentence with a code sample spliced onto the end.
Seven did, and shipped. It is not a check that these lines match their guides
verbatim, because they are not supposed to.

## Why it is shaped this way

Decision records, not guidance. They record reasoning as it stood at a point in
time; reading one as current advice will mislead you. Two were accepted and one
was rejected — and the rejected one is the most useful of the three, because it
carries the measurements that killed the idea.

- [ADR 0001 — the pool frames every message in a versioned binary envelope](https://github.com/AbelVM/performance-helpers/blob/main/adr/0001-versioned-envelope-protocol.md) (Accepted)
- [ADR 0002 — `PowerQueue` is a hand-rolled ring buffer, not an array](https://github.com/AbelVM/performance-helpers/blob/main/adr/0002-ring-buffer-queue.md) (Accepted)
- [ADR 0003 — a frequency admission filter does not earn its keep on this workload](https://github.com/AbelVM/performance-helpers/blob/main/adr/0003-tinylfu-admission-window.md) (Rejected, with the measurements that killed it)
