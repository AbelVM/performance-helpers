# performance-helpers

> A dependency-free toolbox of small, fast, Node/browser libraries: a worker pool, an LRU/TTL cache, rate limiters, a concurrency gate, zero-copy buffer helpers, a versioned message codec, and a tiny logger. No runtime dependencies, ESM + CJS bundles, 1.0.3 published with 2.0 unreleased.

A prompt-oriented index of the documentation. For *how* to use the library start at
the quick guide; for *why* something is shaped the way it is, read the decision
records — they are the reasoning as it stood at a point in time, and reading one
as current guidance will mislead you.

## Optional: why, and what was tried and rejected

Design decisions, kept out of the guides on purpose. Read these when a decision
looks arbitrary and you need to know whether it is load-bearing or accidental.

- [ADR 0001 — the pool frames every message in a versioned binary envelope](https://github.com/AbelVM/performance-helpers/blob/main/adr/0001-versioned-envelope-protocol.md) (Accepted)
- [ADR 0002 — `PowerQueue` is a hand-rolled ring buffer, not an array](https://github.com/AbelVM/performance-helpers/blob/main/adr/0002-ring-buffer-queue.md) (Accepted)
- [ADR 0003 — a frequency admission filter does not earn its keep on this workload](https://github.com/AbelVM/performance-helpers/blob/main/adr/0003-tinylfu-admission-window.md) (Rejected, with the measurements that killed it)
